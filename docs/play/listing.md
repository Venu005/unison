**Full description:**

> Unison connects your Android phone and your Mac directly over your own Wi-Fi. No account, no cloud, nothing to sign up for.
>
> **What you can do**
> • Send files both ways: drop a file on Unison in your Mac's menu bar, or share anything on your phone to "Send to Mac". Big files resume if the connection drops.
> • See your phone's notifications on your Mac, reply to messages and clear them from there.
> • Copy on your Mac and paste on your phone. Send your phone's clipboard to the Mac with one tap.
> • Take calls on your Mac: answer, decline or hang up, and dial numbers or click phone links on the Mac (the call audio stays on your phone).
> • Browse your phone's photos and videos from your Mac, preview them and save them.
> • Mirror your phone's screen to a window on your Mac, with its sound, and control it with your Mac's mouse and keyboard (optional).
> • Ring your phone when it's lost in the sofa, control music, and open links on the other device.
>
> **Private by design**
> Your phone and your Mac talk only to each other, on your local network, encrypted end to end. Unison collects nothing: there's no server, no analytics and no ads.
>
> **You'll need**
> Unison for Mac (free, macOS 14 or later) from venu005.github.io/unison. Open it, scan its QR code with this app, and you're paired.
