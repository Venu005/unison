# App access (App content → App access)

**Choose:** "All or some functionality in my app is restricted". Then add these instructions.

**Name:** Testing Unison with its Mac app

**Instructions (paste):**
> Unison is the phone half of a pair: it needs the free Unison app on a Mac (macOS 14 or later) on the same Wi-Fi network. There's no account or login.
>
> 1. On a Mac, download Unison from https://venu005.github.io/unison/ ("Download for Mac") and drag it to Applications.
> 2. Open it once. macOS says it can't verify the app because it's free and not registered with Apple. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, click "Open Anyway", then confirm. Step-by-step pictures: https://venu005.github.io/unison/#install
> 3. In Unison on the Mac, choose Phone → Pair a Phone. A QR code appears.
> 4. Open Unison on the Android phone. The guided tour starts at "Connect to your Mac": tap "Scan the QR code", allow local network access and the camera, and scan the code.
> 5. The tour continues. Send a photo to the Mac (Send → Photos), turn on notifications (Notifications tab → Turn on notification access → Agree), send the clipboard (Clipboard tab), and optionally turn on Unison control (Settings → Unison control → tick the box → Agree).
> 6. Mirroring: on the Mac, open Unison → Phone → Mirror, then press Start on the phone. With Unison control on, click and type in the Mac window to use the phone.
>
> A video of all of this: ‹link to the end-to-end video from video-scripts.md›

No username or password is needed.
