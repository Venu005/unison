# Store graphics

**Required:**
- App icon, 512×512 PNG. It's already rendered at `website/public/icon-512.png`, from `design/icon/`.
- Feature graphic, 1024×500 PNG or JPG. Use the website's peach hero background with the Unison wordmark and a phone-plus-Mac illustration, but none of the Android robot artwork (Play's trademark rules for store graphics) and no text beyond "Unison". Export it from `website/og/og-image.svg` as a starting point, or capture the hero at 1024×500.
- At least 2 phone screenshots (Play accepts up to 8), 9:16, at least 1080 px on the short side. Take them on the phone with real test content: no real people's names, messages or photos.

**Screens to capture, in this order:**
1. Home: "Connected to ‹Mac›" with Recent activity showing a few transfers.
2. The guided tour bubble on "Send a photo to your Mac".
3. Notifications tab: the app list with mirroring on.
4. Clipboard tab: a few recent items.
5. Settings: Connection, Files, Photos, Remote control and Calls.
6. The notification disclosure sheet. It shows reviewers and users the privacy stance.
7. Optional: a phone-and-Mac photo of mirroring. A device-frame image is fine; don't use Apple logos.

**Keep out of the graphics:** prices, "free" badges overlaid on screenshots, other apps' trademarks, Apple logos and the Android robot.
