Install on your Mac

One minute,
once.

Unison is free and not registered with Apple, so macOS asks you to confirm it the first time. After that it opens like any other app.

  1. 01

    Download

    Download Unison.dmg, open it and drag Unison into Applications.

  2. 02

    Open it once

    Double-click Unison. macOS says it can't verify it. Click Done.

  3. 03

    Open Anyway

    System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, click Open Anyway.

  4. 04

    Confirm

    Click Open Anyway again, then enter your Mac password or use Touch ID.

  5. 05

    Pair your phone

    Allow local network access. Then install the Android app (coming soon to Google Play) and scan the QR code.

Comfortable with Terminal?

Skip steps 2 to 4 with one command:xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Unison.app

Check your download

Compare the file's SHA-256 with the one in the release notes.shasum -a 256 ~/Downloads/Unison.dmg

Why the warning? Apple charges $99 a year to register an app. Unison is a free side project, so it isn't registered. It's open source (MIT): you can read all of the code on GitHub.
