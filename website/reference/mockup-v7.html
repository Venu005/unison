Install on your Mac

One minute,
once.

MacDroid is free and not registered with Apple, so macOS asks you to confirm it the first time. After that it opens like any other app, and updates do too.

SCREENSHOT
01

Download

Open MacDroid.dmg and drag MacDroid into Applications.

SCREENSHOT
02

Open it once

macOS says it can't verify MacDroid. Click Done, not Move to Trash.

SCREENSHOT
03

Open Anyway

System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway.

SCREENSHOT
04

Confirm

Enter your Mac password, then Open Anyway again.

SCREENSHOT
05

Pair your phone

Allow local network access, install the Android app and scan the QR code.

Comfortable with Terminal?

Skip steps 2 to 4 with one command:xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/MacDroid.app

Check your download

Compare the file's SHA-256 with the one published for this version.shasum -a 256 ~/Downloads/MacDroid.dmg
Why the warning? Apple charges $99 a year to register an app. MacDroid is a free side project, so it isn't registered. The code is the same either way, and you can read all of it on GitHub.
